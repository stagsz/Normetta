# SMB light-QM — Sales CTA Variants

Use these in outreach, landing follow-up, or follow-on nurture. Each variant
keeps the same evidence-core promise but frames it for a different buyer role.

## Variant A: Compliance leads
Subject: ISO 9001 readiness without adding headcount
Opening: Most SMBs already produce 60–80% of the evidence an auditor needs.
Offer: Normetta light-QM review to map existing records to clauses and surface gaps before your next audit.
CTA: Reply to book a 20-minute gap-analysis walk-through.

## Variant B: Founders / operators
Subject: Pass the audit without hiring a quality manager
Opening: You don’t need a full QM department to prove your system works.
Offer: Light-QM workflow built for small teams—templates, evidence mapping, and PDF export.
CTA: Request a quick demo or start the checklist version.

## Variant C: Procurement-driven buyers
Subject: Supplier audit readiness your customers can verify
Opening: Enterprise buyers increasingly require ISO 9001 evidence from suppliers.
Offer: Normetta turns your existing SOPs, tickets, and records into a coherent audit package.
CTA: Ask for a sample output or export example.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim automatic certification.
- Do not promise AI-generated compliance conclusions.
- Keep claims evidence-based and typical to SME operation.
