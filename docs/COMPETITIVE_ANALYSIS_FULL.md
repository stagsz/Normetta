## 6. Winning Moves (Recommended Strategy)

### Move 1: Own "Self-Assessment First" Positioning
- **Tactic:** Lead every marketing message with self-assessment wizard — nothing else in market does this
- **Proof point:** "Start your ISO 9001 journey in 10 minutes, not 10 weeks"
- **Against Compliance Automation:** "We're purpose-built for ISO 9001, not an add-on to SOC 2"
- **Against Traditional QMS:** "Same CAPA/audit power. 1/10th the price. Self-serve onboarding."

### Move 2: Price-Wedge Upmarket
- **Current:** €588 (Starter) → €1,788 (Pro) for 10-50 users
- **Opportunity:** Add "Enterprise" at €4,990/mo (unlimited users + all modules + dedicated support)
- **Anchoring:** €4,990 vs MasterControl at $25K+ and ETQ at $25K+ = still 5-10x cheaper
- **Features needed:** Multi-site, SSO/SAML, API access, custom templates, SLA

### Move 3: Certification Body Partnership Program
- **Partner:** 3-5 certification bodies in Sweden/EU
- **Program:** AuditFlow "Certification Ready" badge + streamlined handoff
- **Revenue:** Referral fees ($500-2,000 per certified customer)
- **Defensibility:** Creates switching cost (cert body trained on AuditFlow data export)

### Move 4: Module-Based Upsell (Land-and-Expand)
- **Free:** Core self-assessment (5 users)
- **Starter €49/mo:** NCR/CAPA, audit planning
- **Professional €149/mo:** Advanced reports, evidence storage, APIs
- **Enterprise:** All modules (supplier, training, document control v2, ESG)
- **Add-on à-la-carte:** Supplier mgmt (+€29/mo), Training mgmt (+€19/mo), Document Pro (+€29/mo)

### Move 5: Build "ISO 9001 Readiness Score" Lead Magnet
- **Tool:** Free web app → 10-question diagnostic → score + report
- **CTA:** "Get your score → unlock AuditFlow trial with pre-filled gaps"
- **Distribution:** SEO ("ISO 9001 readiness assessment"), LinkedIn, certification body co-marketing
